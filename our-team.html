Meet our team

— meet the team —

We bring over 20 years of broad experience to our clients. Our team includes lawyers with backgrounds in Polish, English and US law. What connects us is our shared belief that law may be a catalyst for innovation and growth and not just a set of rules and limitations.

01

Founder and Partner

Barbara Sobowska

Barbara is a cross-border transactional lawyer advising tech companies, founders, and investors on venture capital, M&A, and growth transactions across the US, UK, and Europe. She is qualified in New York and in England and Wales, and works with clients on fundraising, investment structures, shareholder relations, and international expansion.

Her practice spans domestic and cross-border VC, M&A, and strategic corporate matters, helping clients structure deals, navigate growth, and operate confidently across jurisdictions. Before founding MOST Partners in 2016, Barbara worked in leading international law firms including Weil, Gotshal & Manges, White & Case, and Wilmer Hale in London, advising institutional investors, global corporates, and high-growth companies.

Barbara holds Master of Laws degrees from Harvard Law School (US) and the Jagiellonian University (Poland), and completed further legal studies at Katholieke Universiteit Leuven (Belgium) and Bucerius Law School (Germany).

Areas of Expertise

  • Venture Capital and Growth Financing
  • M&A and Strategic Transactions
  • Shareholder Agreements and Joint Ventures
  • Corporate Structuring
  • Commercial and Corporate Advisory
  • Cross-Border Expansion
  • Venture Debt
  • Incentive Programs (ESOPs and VSOPs — virtual/phantom programs)
02

Partner

Jacek Balicki

Jacek is a transactional attorney advising on M&A, corporate structuring, and complex cross-border transactions for private equity, venture capital, corporate clients, and high-net-worth individuals. He brings over a decade of experience executing sophisticated deals across multiple jurisdictions and is recognised for his clear communication, strategic perspective, and "truly excellent execution capabilities" (Chambers Global).

His practice includes share and asset deals, investment agreements, shareholder arrangements, reorganisations, and a wide range of corporate and commercial matters. Jacek also has deep experience in transaction insurance (W&I), acting for both insurers and insured parties.

Before joining MOST Partners, Jacek spent more than ten years in the transactional teams of Weil, Gotshal & Manges and Baker McKenzie, and worked at PwC in Warsaw, where he advised on domestic and cross-border M&A transactions — both public and private.

Jacek holds an LL.M. from the Catholic University of America and a Master of Laws from the Jagiellonian University.

Areas of Expertise

  • M&A and Strategic Transactions
  • Corporate Structuring
  • Corporate Reorganisations and Restructuring
  • Venture Capital
  • Corporate and Commercial Advisory
  • Transaction Insurance (W&I)
03

Transactional Counsel

Karolina Bressa

Karolina is a transactional attorney advising companies, founders, and investors on venture capital, M&A, and corporate reorganisations. She has several years of experience supporting entrepreneurs and high-growth businesses, working both as external counsel and as an in-house lawyer.

Her practice spans a wide range of corporate and commercial matters, including company formations, venture capital investments, shareholder arrangements, and key commercial agreements. Karolina also has a strong background in banking and finance, where she advises borrowers and lenders on financing arrangements and security documentation.

She works closely with clients on corporate structuring, growth transactions, and strategic collaborations, combining corporate and commercial experience with a practical, business-oriented perspective. Her work supports companies at various stages of development, from early formation through expansion, reorganisations, and investment processes.

Before joining MOST Partners, Karolina gained experience in investment funds and international law firms, including Coast2Coast Capital, CMS, and DLA Piper. Karolina holds a Master of Laws degree from Adam Mickiewicz University (Poland).

Areas of Expertise

  • Venture Capital

  • Due Diligence
  • Incentive Programs (ESOPs and VSOPs — virtual/phantom programs)
  • Day-to-Day Operations
  • Banking and Finance

Corporate Counsel

Justyna Szuprowska

Justyna is a corporate attorney focusing on corporate structuring, reorganisations, regulatory compliance, and day-to-day corporate governance. She has extensive experience supporting organisations from the inside, having spent more than a decade as in-house counsel within a major multinational corporate group.

Her practice covers company formations, mergers, demergers, transformations, asset and share transactions, and a wide range of commercial agreements. She routinely advises on corporate governance, regulatory compliance for private and public companies, and supports clients through due diligence processes in M&A and investment transactions.

Justyna combines deep corporate knowledge with a practical, business-oriented approach shaped by her years in an operational legal environment. Before joining MOST Partners, she worked for over 10 years in the corporate legal department at Pelion.

Areas of Expertise

  • Corporate Structuring
  • Corporate Reorganisations and Transformations
  • Corporate Governance and Commercial Advisory
  • Regulatory and Compliance
  • Due Diligence
  • M&A and Transaction Support
05

Associate

Dominika Jakuszewska

Dominika is a transaction-focused associate advising clients on private M&A, venture capital transactions, and corporate matters, with a strong emphasis on intellectual property and technology-related issues. She supports clients throughout the entire transaction process — from due diligence to drafting and negotiating key agreements, including investment agreements, shareholders' agreements, and management arrangements.

Her experience includes advising on company formations, capital contributions, management agreements, and incentive schemes, as well as commercial and corporate documentation. Dominika also works on IP and technology matters, including IP due diligence, licensing arrangements, and commercial exploitation of technology — helping clients ensure that IP assets are properly protected, assigned, and structured throughout the corporate lifecycle.

Dominika brings an international perspective, supported by academic experience in Poland, France, and the UK. Before joining MOST Partners, she gained experience at Polish law firms. She holds a Master of Laws from the Jagiellonian University and a BA in International Relations (with distinction), and completed additional studies at the University of Warsaw and Sciences Po Paris.

Areas of Expertise

  • Venture Capital
  • Corporate and Commercial Advisory
  • Intellectual Property and Technology Transactions
  • Competition and Consumer Law
  • Corporate Governance and Commercial Advisory
  • Regulatory and Compliance
  • Due Diligence
  • M&A and Transaction Support
06

Associate

Wojciech Młodawski

Wojciech is a transaction-focused associate advising on venture capital, M&A, and corporate matters, combining legal expertise with a strong financial background and hands-on experience in the venture capital industry. His practice integrates legal, financial, and operational perspectives — shaped by more than three years working inside a VC fund, where he coordinated deals end-to-end and managed a broad range of investment and operational processes.

He specialises in venture financing, share and asset deals, corporate advisory, and due diligence. At Arkley Brinc VC, Wojciech served as COO & Investment Manager, overseeing transactions from due diligence to closing, supporting portfolio companies, and managing organisational and investment matters. This experience gives him a unique understanding of how investors evaluate risk, structure deals, and make decisions.

Before joining MOST Partners, Wojciech gained additional experience at international law firms including CMS and Greenberg Traurig.

Wojciech's academic background spans both law and finance. He holds degrees in Law and Business Administration from the University of Warsaw and an MA in Finance and Accounting from SGH Warsaw School of Economics. He also completed programmes at the University of Bologna and Columbia University, and received a special award in the national Commercial Law Review & Wolters Kluwer competition for best master's theses.

Areas of Expertise

  • Venture Capital
  • Corporate and Commercial Advisory
  • Due Diligence
  • M&A and Transaction Support
  • Corporate Governance and Commercial Advisory
07

Tax Counsel

Anna Zięba

Anna is a tax counsel with over a decade of experience advising domestic and international clients on tax aspects of M&A, financing, reorganisations, compensation structures, and cross-border operations. She supports clients in structuring transactions, managing tax risk, and ensuring compliance in complex regulatory environments.

Her expertise includes tax structuring for investments, employee compensation and incentive plans, financing and refinancing, withholding tax, secondments, and day-to-day tax advisory. Anna also advises on CRS/FATCA compliance and MDR reporting, and is an author and speaker on tax matters.

Anna worked in the tax teams of leading Polish law firms and at PwC in Warsaw.

Anna holds an LL.M. from UC Berkeley School of Law and a Master of Laws from the Jagiellonian University.

Areas of Expertise

  • Tax support in M&A transactions
  • Tax structuring of reorganisations and restructurings
  • Tax structuring of management and employee compensation
  • Tax advisory for financial transactions
  • MDR reporting
  • CRS & FATCA compliance
  • Ongoing tax advisory
