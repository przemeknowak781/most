Company Incorporation and Structuring

We help set up and structure businesses — locally and internationally — so you are ready to scale and raise capital.

  • Company incorporation (local and international)
  • Founders' agreements
  • Cap table planning
  • Group structuring and reorganisation
  • Legal frameworks for operational scalability and investor-readiness

Funding and Investment Rounds

We support you through every stage of fundraising — from term sheets to closing — so your deals are clear, fair, and future-proof.

  • Term sheets planning and structuring
  • SAFE and convertible instruments
  • Equity rounds (pre-seed to Series A/B+)
  • Debt structuring (venture debt, loans and credit facilities)
  • Due diligence preparation and investor readiness checks
  • Shareholders' and investment agreements
  • Investor negotiations
  • Governance design and post-investment structure

Hiring and Talent

We cover the legal side of hiring, equity plans, and working with contractors — so you can build the right team with confidence.

  • Engagement terms planning and structuring
  • Employment law and employment contracts
  • Contractor, advisor, and consultant agreements
  • Structuring and implementation of incentive plans
  • Cross-border hiring and compliance alignment
  • Policies, handbooks, onboarding documentation

Commercial Contracts and Day-to-Day Operations

From everyday business agreements and client contracts to commercial partnerships and collaborations, we draft and negotiate contracts that support your product, platform, and business operations.

  • Day-to-day corporate affairs and governance
  • Services agreements, licensing, and commercial contracts
  • Terms of use, privacy policies, and user-facing documents
  • Legal support for product development and go-to-market
  • Client, partner, and supplier agreements
  • Joint ventures and strategic collaborations
  • Data protection and GDPR compliance
  • Operational policies and internal governance processes

IP and Technology

We help you protect what you build — covering IP ownership, licensing, confidentiality, and open-source risk.

  • IP strategy and ownership structuring (founder, team, contractors)
  • Software licensing, IP assignment, and confidentiality agreements (NDAs)
  • Trademark filings and brand protection (via partners if needed)
  • Support in tech transfer or open-source risk analysis

Cross-Border Expansion

We guide your international growth, helping structure foreign entities, review global contracts, and manage compliance.

  • Legal structuring for international growth
  • Establishment of foreign entities or subsidiaries
  • Coordination with local counsel and cross-border compliance
  • Review of cross-border contracts and risk allocation
  • Employment, tax, and commercial alignment with local regulations
  • Market entry strategies and operational frameworks

M&A, Exits and Strategic Transactions

We support buy- and sell-side deals, drafting and negotiating documents and managing post-deal transitions.

  • Legal due diligence (buy-side or sell-side)
  • Asset deals
  • Share deals
  • Transaction document drafting and negotiation
  • SPAs, shareholders' agreements, earn-outs, and warranties
  • Coordination with financial, tax, and regulatory teams
  • Post-deal integration (employment, IP, governance, operations)
  • Corporate restructurings ahead of sale or acquisition

Ongoing Legal Support

As your business evolves, we stay with you — offering fast, practical, and tailored advice without unnecessary complexity.

  • Legal support that adapts as your business scales
  • Business-driven solutions for daily operations
  • Practical guidance balancing risk and momentum
  • Available when needed — without unnecessary overhead or complexity

How We Work?

We are driven by a passion for making things happen. That is why we:

  1. Deliver business-driven legal solutions

    Always thinking two steps ahead. We focus on what matters now, while anticipating what comes next to protect your future and support your momentum.

  2. Structure legal frameworks

    We ensure your legal setup works for your business — enabling smooth operations, informed decisions, and sustainable growth.

  3. Draft with clarity, precision, and purpose

    Always driven by what matters most. Our documents are designed to be understood and used, and focused on the underlying objectives of your team, your partners, your investors — whoever needs to act on them.

  4. Negotiate with a focus

    We stay focused on your priorities and outcomes — creating value, protecting your interests, and keeping deals on track.

  5. Close deals seamlessly

    We coordinate across borders, time zones, and teams to keep transactions moving smoothly from negotiation to closing.

  6. Stay by your side

    Supporting your business in what comes next. Your business evolves, and so does our support. We're not just here for the deal — we are your long-term partner in law and business.
